# How Signet (`BLINCS-48-X`) protects your results

Signet computes `BLINCS-48-X` signatures matching [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md) with zero `unsafe` Rust and minimal runtime dependencies (`blake3-servil-core = "=0.11.0"`, `constant_time_eq = "=0.4.2"`, and `getrandom = "=0.4.3"`, strictly single-threaded with zero background worker threads), paired with standalone `#![no_std]` zero-alloc, zero-coding-table verifier crate `crates/signet-verify`. Verification is fail-closed (`Result<(), InvalidImprint>` / `Result<(), VerifyError>` / `Result<(), StreamVerifyError>`), and when an internal check detects a broken contract, the call panics rather than returning a silently broken signature.

## Timing and secret independence

Signet is written so that secret material does not steer branches or memory indices in its Rust code:

1. **Signing branches and indices depend only on public or reconstructible values.** The `U-PORS` leaf selections, Octopus sibling topology, codeword digits, hypertree address (`leaf_addr`), Merkle sibling directions, and `WOTS+C` chain step counts are all derived from `D_seed`, `salt`, `randomizer`, `ctx`, `ctr_ft`, `ctr_l`, and tree roots that are reconstructible from the message, context, and imprint. (For confidential messages before publication, chain step counts vary with message-derived tree roots.)
2. **Secret material is only copied or hashed.** `seed`, `SecretKey`, `salt_key`, `randomizer_key`, `run_key`, `keygen_key`, `draw`, and unrevealed `U-PORS`/`WOTS+C` chain starts are never branched on, sorted by, or used as array indices; they are passed directly into `blake3-servil-core`.
3. **Underlying hash kernels and hardware limits.** `blake3-servil-core` symbolically proves its assembly kernels branch- and address-independent of keys and inputs, while leaving CPU data-independent timing modes (Arm `DIT`, Intel `DOITM`) to the calling thread or OS. Power/frequency side channels (such as Hertzbleed) and data-dependent prefetchers without `DIT` (such as GoFetch on Apple M1–M3) sit outside software proofs; callers requiring hardware timing guarantees should enable `DIT`/`DOITM` at the thread or platform level.

## Bugs these steps found

Concrete defects found during development, mutation testing, and cross-family reviews, and the checks that keep them from returning:

1. **Recycled reader buffer bytes visible to `Read::read` (Round 2 review).** Fixed by eliminating recycled reader buffers entirely in favor of the caller-pushed `Sink` API.
2. **Imprinting self-check did not independently bind `imprint[..16]` to `short[..16]` (Round 2 review).** Fixed in `SignetId::verify_short`; guarded by `test::verify_short_rejects_mismatched_salt_against_short` and planted-fault control #6 in `tools/controls.py`.
3. **Unchecked `u64::MAX` stream length (Round 2 review).** Fixed via `checked_add_stream_len`; guarded by `test::checked_constructors_reject_out_of_range_values` and planted-fault control #11.
4. **Reference validation skipped under `python3 -O` (Round 2 review).** Replaced `assert` with explicit `ValueError`/`TypeError` checks in `reference/blincs.py`; guarded by the `python3 -O` check in `tools/land.py` (which also refuses `-O` itself).
5. **Missing `#[must_use]` on verification methods (Round 3 review).** Fixed with `#[must_use]` annotations and fail-closed `Result` return types.
6. **Concurrent partial-file visibility & transitive `std` leakage in `#![no_std]` WASM (Astra v2 audit & freestanding WASM harness).** Fixed atomic state rotation via sibling temp file + POSIX `rename` / no-replace `hard_link`, and fixed `constant_time_eq` `default-features = false`; guarded continuously by `wasm-test/run_wasm.mjs` (0 WASM imports).
7. **Float64 cancellation in hypergeometric inclusion-exclusion & untested `sibs == 45` / `v == 376` boundaries (`tools/optimize.py` + release `cargo-mutants`).** Replaced alternating inclusion-exclusion with exact positive hypergeometric DP and added exact `sibs == 45` vs `46` and `v == 376` boundary tests, bringing surviving unexpected mutants to **0**.
8. **Code-first pre-filtering on independent `FEW_CODE` input (formal ROM proof audit).** Eliminated at zero cost (`-4 B` input) by chaining `FEW_CODE` directly from the 32-byte `FEW_SELECT` output `H_sel` across `SPEC.md`, Rust, Python, TypeScript, and Lean 4.
